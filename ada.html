Accessibility Statement

Last updated January 30, 2025

Functional Cognitive Rehab is committed to ensuring accessibility for all visitors, including individuals with disabilities. We continuously work to improve the user experience for everyone and apply the relevant accessibility standards to enhance usability.

Compliance Standards

Our website strives to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at the AA level. These guidelines define how to make web content more accessible to individuals with a wide range of disabilities, including visual, auditory, cognitive, and motor impairments. Key areas of compliance include:

  • Ensuring text contrast ratios for readability
  • Implementing keyboard navigation compatibility
  • Structuring content with clear and descriptive headings
  • Offering resizable text without loss of content or functionality
  • Designing content to be understandable and navigable by screen readers
  • Providing descriptive alternative text for images and multimedia elements

Ongoing Efforts

We conduct regular accessibility audits, use automated testing tools, and incorporate user feedback to improve our website's accessibility. Our team is actively working to identify and resolve potential accessibility barriers.
