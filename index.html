Who we help

Do any of these symptoms feel familiar?

After suffering a brain injury, many people unfortunately experience new difficulty with concentration, memory, problem solving, and word-finding. These things aren’t personality traits and they are not permanent — they’re symptoms, and they respond to treatment.

Reading the same paragraph three times

You reach the bottom of the page and realize none of it stayed with you.

Losing your train of thought mid-sentence

The word was right there. Now the whole room is waiting on you.

Everything takes twice as long

Tasks that used to be automatic now demand full concentration — and drain you.

Falling behind at work or school

You know you're capable of more. Keeping up has quietly become the whole job.

Forgetting names, plans, appointments

You write everything down and things still slip through.

Being told you "look fine"

The injury doesn't show. The difficulty is real, measurable — and treatable.

Running out of mental energy by noon

A morning of thinking wipes you out for the rest of the day.

Small decisions feel enormous

Planning, choosing, starting — the executive gears grind where they used to spin.

Recognize yourself in two or more? An assessment is worth your time.

A combination of restorative and compensatory techniques that improve attention, memory, organization, problem-solving, planning and verbal expression.

✓ Built around your real goals — your job, your classes, your life
